How this is scored

Price per acre: median of the land listings in your app with plots of ¼–50 acres, so tiny building plots don't skew it (the middle half is shown on hover). Countries with fewer than 20 such plots fall back to all their listings and are drawn dashed: treat their height as rough.

Weather: daily data (2016–2025, Open-Meteo/ERA5; 2021–2025 for countries added without listings, at their main sea and ski areas) at up to three spots where that country's listings actually are, averaged for the months you pick. Each month scores daytime highs (best 20–27 °C), rainy days and sunshine. With ⛷ on, snowy months (high ≤ 5 °C, 4+ snow days) score on snow instead of warmth.

Quality of life: UN Human Development Index 2023 (health, education, income).

Income tax (bubble size): the top personal rate if you live there, either on income earned in that country or, with 🌍, on income from abroad (investments, remote work). 0% = territorial or foreign income exempt. Many countries give new arrivals a few tax-free years on foreign income (UK, NZ, Japan, Chile, Indonesia, Portugal's IFICI); those are in the notes, the chart uses the long-run rate. Rough figures for 2026: confirm with a tax adviser, and note US citizens owe US tax wherever they live.

Tax residency: with the days slider and the 🏡 box, each country's rules decide whether you'd be tax-resident. If you're not, it doesn't tax your income from abroad (🌍 bubble = 0%); income earned inside a country is taxed there either way. Tests that average over several years (USA, Ireland, Norway, Mauritius) are shown as the per-year figure if you come every year. A home can make you resident with far fewer days (Germany: any day; Austria: over 70 days; UK: from 121 days), and a treaty between two countries can override this. The rules are summarised in the table; confirm with an adviser before relying on a day count.

Property purchase tax (hover only): what a foreign buyer of a ~US$1M second home pays over 10 years: purchase taxes + foreign-buyer surcharges + 10 years of annual property/wealth tax, as a % of the price. Rules vary by region inside countries, so treat it as a guide and confirm locally. Foreign-ownership bans are in the notes; they aren't taxes but can rule a country out.

Score = 40% life + weather, 30% cheapness (log price), 30% low income tax. Dashed (non-comparable) prices count as average cheapness.
