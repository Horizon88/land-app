Khao Thong — Site Layout Options

Three arrangements for the ~23-rai parcel (deeds 54240 + 50861), each with the main home, a source pond, a waterfall descending the slope, two retaining ponds, and separated walking / driving paths. All are oriented to the real site: west access road, sea view to the south (downhill), high ground (~70 m) to the north, road/low ground (~14 m) to the south. Water always flows downhill — from the home's pond toward the view.

⚙ Engineering reality, all three: the "waterfall" is a recirculating feature — the two retaining ponds store water and a pump returns it to the source pond; they also double as stormwater catchment for runoff coming down a 23% slope (genuinely useful, not just decorative). Exact pad elevations, pond sizes, and the drive gradient get fixed once the 1 m topo survey is in — these are concept arrangements to react to, not construction drawings. North = high/back, south = downhill toward the sea view; every plan keeps the home on the view ridge and lets water fall toward the water.
