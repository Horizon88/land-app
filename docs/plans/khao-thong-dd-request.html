1Title verification & chain of ownership

  • Verify each Chanote (Nor Sor 4 Jor) is genuine and current against the original at the Krabi Provincial Land Office — not just the copy provided by the seller/agent.
  • Confirm the exact registered owner(s) of each deed, the acquisition date, and the chain of title (how long held, prior transfers).
  • Confirm the deed on file matches the physical parcel (deed no., land no., survey/tambon no., map sheet).

2Encumbrances & charges

  • Search the reverse of each Chanote and the Land Office register for: mortgages, liens, court seizures/injunctions, caveats, and any registered lease, usufruct (สิทธิเก็บกิน), superficies, or servitude (ภาระจำยอม).
  • Confirm the land is free of any third-party occupation rights or tenant/plantation claims.

3Seller identity & authority to sell

  • Verify the seller's legal identity and their authority to sell each deed. If an individual, confirm spousal consent where required; if selling under power of attorney, verify the POA is valid and specific.
  • If any parcel is held in a Thai company, conduct corporate due diligence: shareholders, directors, authorised signatories, financials, and any nominee-shareholding exposure that would taint title or complicate a clean transfer to us.

4Boundary & survey

  • Commission (or arrange with the Land Office) a formal cadastral boundary survey (รังวัด) to confirm the registered area matches reality, locate the boundary markers (หลักเขต), and identify any encroachment — by neighbours onto the land, or by the land onto public/forest/foreshore.
  • Overlay the surveyed boundary against the DOL parcel map and the seller's marketing outline; report any discrepancy.

5Legal access

High priority. Confirm each parcel has legally registered access to a public road (the Sao Krabi 2007 / the coastal highway) — an enforceable right, not merely a physical track. If access crosses third-party land, confirm a registered servitude / right-of-way (ภาระจำยอม) exists or can be secured, and that it is adequate for construction traffic and future use.

6Zoning & land classification

  • Confirm the parcels' zone under the Krabi Comprehensive Plan (ผังเมืองรวมกระบี่) and the permitted uses (residential / hotel / density / setbacks / height).
  • Confirm the land is full private freehold (Chanote) and not Sor Por Kor (agricultural, non-sellable) or otherwise restricted.

7Protected areas & environmental status

  • Confirm the parcels are not within, or overlapping, a national park, wildlife sanctuary, forest reserve (ป่าสงวนแห่งชาติ), mangrove conservation, or the foreshore/public-waterway zone — Ao Thalane karst has conservation overlays.
  • Advise on EIA / EEA applicability for our intended build, given coastal proximity, hillside slope (~23% average), and project scale (room count / rai thresholds under the MoNRE notifications).

8Building & slope regulations

  • Advise on hillside/slope building restrictions (Krabi limits or bans construction on slopes above set gradients) and any coastal setback / building-height limits near the shoreline.
  • Confirm what building footprint and height are permissible on the buildable terraces.

9Utilities

  • Confirm the legal availability of mains electricity (PEA) and water to the parcels, and any easements required to bring services in.

10Foreign-ownership structure

Please advise. As a foreign buyer I cannot hold Thai land in my own name. Advise the correct, fully compliant acquisition structure for a ~23.5-rai holding — Thai Limited Company (with genuine, non-nominee Thai shareholding), registered long lease, BOI, or Thai-spouse ownership — with the risks, tax, and control implications of each, and your recommendation. We will not proceed via an illegal nominee arrangement.

11Taxes & transfer costs

  • Estimate total closing costs and their allocation: transfer fee (2%), Specific Business Tax (3.3%) or stamp duty (0.5%), withholding tax, and any others; plus ongoing Land & Building Tax.

12Assembly mechanics & contract protection

  • The primary purchase is two deeds that must transfer together (54240 + 50861). Confirm whether they share one owner or two, and structure the contract so completion is conditional on clean transfer of both — we will not complete on one alone.
  • Recommend contract protections: subject-to-DD and subject-to-survey conditions, deposit held in escrow / by counsel, and clear default/refund terms.
  • Report any pending litigation, boundary dispute, or third-party claim affecting any parcel.

13Deliverables

  • A written title & due-diligence report per parcel (title status, encumbrances, access, zoning, environmental, structure recommendation) with a clear go / no-go.
  • The boundary survey result and the recommended acquisition structure.
  • Your estimate of fees and the timeline to complete the above before we release any deposit.
